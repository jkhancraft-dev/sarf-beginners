Available exact textbook example/exercise answers contribute additional individual forms. Reviewed Ism al-Fāʿil and Ism al-Mafʿūl bases can be tested as masculine/feminine singular, dual, and sound plural forms.

## Accuracy and coverage boundary

The existing reviewed complete-paradigm registry has **14 representative verbs**, not all 287 catalog entries. Therefore, the app does not claim a complete paradigm for every verb. For each selected verb, the app uses only exact forms already supported by the uploaded textbook lesson examples, exercise-answer data, or the reviewed paradigm registry. It displays a limited-coverage notice where individual conjugation data is absent. Root, wazan, and meaning questions remain available for catalog entries without stored form questions. Missing forms are not generated by guesswork.

The uploaded textbook describes patterns and provides exercise examples. This V10.6 feature uses those available records; it does not silently treat a verb's root/pattern alone as proof of its full active/passive conjugation or maṣdar.
